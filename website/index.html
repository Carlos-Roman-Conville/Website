About

Why I Founded CRC Solutions

Everywhere I worked, I saw the same pattern: capable people losing hours to repetitive tasks, disconnected tools, missed follow-ups, and processes that depended too heavily on someone remembering the next step.

The problem was rarely that people weren't working hard enough. Their systems were making the work harder than it needed to be.

I founded CRC Solutions because I knew there had to be a better way. Today, I help small businesses identify the operational problems slowing them down and turn those problems into practical systems that save time, capture more opportunities, and make the business easier to run.

And I don't sell anything I don't use myself — CRC Solutions runs on the same AI systems we build for clients. We eat our own cooking, so the technology is proven before it ever reaches you.

You work directly with me — from diagnosing the problem through building, testing, and launching the solution.

Carlos Roman-Conville Founder, CRC Solutions
