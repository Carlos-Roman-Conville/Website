Privacy

How we handle your information

Last updated November 13, 2026

CRC Solutions runs this website, its chat assistant and its contact form. This page explains what we keep, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted. Questions go to carlos@crc-solutions.org.

The chat assistant is AI

The chat on this site is an automated assistant, not a person. It runs on Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. What you type in the chat is sent to Anthropic to produce the replies. It can make mistakes, so please check anything important with us. You can ask for a person at any time with the "Talk to Carlos" button.

What we collect

  • Chat conversations: the messages you and the assistant exchange, the page you opened the chat on, and the time.
  • Details you give us: your name, email address, phone number, business and what you need, when you type them in the chat, the contact form or the "Talk to Carlos" form.
  • Bookings: if you book a consultation, the time and the details above go into our calendar and a calendar invitation is sent to your email.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used to limit abuse (how many chats and messages one connection can send) and appears in our server logs. The counters are deleted within a few days and the logs within 30 days. A Cloudflare check confirms the chat is used by a person, not a bot.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Anthropic (the AI model behind the chat). Anthropic says it does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models by default, and deletes it from its systems within 30 days.
  • Cloudflare (hosts this website, checks for bots, and counts visits without cookies).
  • Google (our calendar and email, when you book or write to us; the website also loads its fonts from Google).
  • Pushover (sends Carlos a phone alert with your name and email when you ask to talk to him).

How long we keep it

  • Chat conversations: deleted automatically after 12 months.
  • Your contact details and what you asked for: kept while we are talking or working together, and deleted when you ask.
  • Chat history in your browser: kept only for the current tab and gone when you close it. "New chat" clears it sooner. This does not delete our copy; see below for that.

Deleting your information

Email carlos@crc-solutions.org from the address you gave us and ask us to delete your information. We will delete your conversations and contact details from our systems and confirm by email, usually within a week. You can also ask for a copy of what we hold about you.

Children

This site is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
