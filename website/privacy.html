How we handle your information

Last updated [Rex to fill: date approved]

CRC Solutions runs this website, its chat assistant, its contact form and its email. This page explains what we keep, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted. Questions go to carlos@crc-solutions.org.

The chat assistant is AI

The chat on this site is an automated assistant, not a person. It runs on Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. What you type in the chat is sent to Anthropic to produce the replies. It can make mistakes, so please check anything important with us. You can ask for a person at any time with the "Talk to Carlos" button.

What we collect

  • Chat conversations: the messages you and the assistant exchange, the page you opened the chat on, and the time.
  • Details you give us: your name, email address and what you need, when you fill in the contact form or the "Talk to Carlos" form, and anything else you choose to type in the chat, such as a phone number or your business name.
  • Bookings: if you book a consultation, the time and the details above go into our calendar and a calendar invitation is sent to your email.
  • Emails you send us: we read them, and an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) may draft our reply. Email addresses and phone numbers are removed from the text before it goes to the AI, and a person reads and approves every reply before it is sent.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used to limit abuse (how many chats and messages one connection can send) and appears in our server logs. The counters are deleted within a few days and the logs within 30 days. A Cloudflare check confirms the chat is used by a person, not a bot.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Businesses we contact

We may email or visit local businesses that could use our services. To find them, we use public business information: state and city business and license registers, the federal NPI registry for healthcare providers, open map data (Overture Maps), ratings from Google, and the contact details a business publishes on its own website. We keep the business name, address, category, and its published email, phone and website, plus notes on our contact with it.

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. If you use it, or reply asking us to stop, we stop emailing that address. We keep the address on a do-not-contact list so we never email it again.

  • Pushover (sends Carlos a phone alert when you ask to talk to him, with your name, email, your note and your last few chat messages).

  • Chat conversations: deleted automatically after 12 months. If you ask to talk to Carlos, a copy of the conversation goes with your contact details and is kept with them (see the next item).

  • Emails: [Rex to fill: how long emails and our AI drafting records are kept.]
  • Do-not-contact list: kept for as long as we send email, so we can honour it.
  • Backups: copies of our business records are kept on our own computer, not in the cloud, for up to 7 months. Something deleted from our records disappears from the backups as they age out.
  • Chat history in your browser: kept only for the current tab and gone when you close it. "New chat" clears it sooner. This does not delete our copy; see below for that.

Deleting your information

Email carlos@crc-solutions.org from the address you gave us and ask us to delete your information. Children

This site is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
