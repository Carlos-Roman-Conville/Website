The Founder

Carlos Roman-Conville

Carlos Roman-Conville spent years managing operations across industries — coordinating logistics across regions at 1-800-GOT-JUNK, building technical systems for live entertainment at Beat the Bomb, and leading teams in the Army Reserve as a combat medic with the 338th Medical Brigade.

At every job, he saw the same problems: missed follow-ups, disconnected tools, repetitive work that kept good businesses stuck. When he started learning to build technology himself, he realized he could fix these problems directly.

CRC Solutions exists because small businesses deserve practical technology built around how they actually work — not expensive platforms that force them to change, and not consultants who hand over a report and leave.

We diagnose the problem, build the solution, and stay to make sure it keeps working.
