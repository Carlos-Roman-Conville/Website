How the Guarantee Works

“If it doesn't make money, it doesn't cost money.”

That's not just a slogan — it's built into how every project runs.

  1. We define success together. Before development begins, we agree on exactly what the system must do and how it will be tested.
  2. A deposit starts standard projects. It reserves your spot and covers the build.
  3. Final payment is due only after the system passes the tests we agreed on. If it doesn't do what we said it would, you don't pay for it.

You know what success means before we build — and you never make the final payment for a system that hasn't met those requirements.
