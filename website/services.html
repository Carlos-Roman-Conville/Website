The AI Receptionist

Stop sending callers to voicemail. Inquiries get answered by phone, chat, or text, including nights and weekends. Appointments get booked, urgent jobs get routed to you, and messages come through when someone needs a human.

The AI Sales Rep

Stop losing leads to slow follow-up. New inquiries get a fast response, quotes get chased instead of forgotten, and your pipeline stays visible so nothing sits untouched while you're on a job.

The AI Account Manager

Keep the customers you already earned. Check-ins after the job, review requests at the right moment, no-show recovery, and win-back outreach for customers who haven't called in a while.

The AI Marketing Manager

Get found. Look professional. Your website, content, social posts, and search presence, handled and kept current instead of untouched since launch.

The AI Accountant

Get paid faster and know where you stand. Invoices go out on time, payments get tracked, expenses get categorized, and you get financial reports without building them yourself.

What these roles do and don't do. CRC's AI roles handle defined, repeatable responsibilities. They do not duplicate every responsibility, judgment, or authority of a human employee. The AI Accountant handles invoicing and bookkeeping, not tax filing, which stays with your accountant, and not payroll compliance, which stays with your payroll provider. The AI Marketing Manager runs your online presence, but it can't take photographs, attend events, or build in-person relationships. Where you already have someone in a seat, we build the repetitive part so that person can focus on the work that actually needs a human.
